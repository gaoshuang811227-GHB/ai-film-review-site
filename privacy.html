AI Film Review Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Data accessed

AI Film Review requests only the Google Drive drive.file permission. It uses this permission only for files created by the application or files that the user explicitly makes available to it. It does not request full Google Drive access or use drive, drive.readonly, or equivalent whole-Drive permissions.

Purpose

Google Drive data is used to upload specific media for review, retrieve those exact files, verify their identity and integrity, and make authorized media available to the internal review workflow.

Storage and sharing

OAuth client credentials and authorization tokens used by the local Runner are stored outside the public source repository. On Windows, locally stored OAuth credentials and tokens are protected using CurrentUser DPAPI.

AI Film Review does not sell Google user data. It does not make Google Drive files publicly accessible as part of its normal workflow. Media is accessed only as required for the authorized internal review workflow.

Revocation

You can revoke the application's access through your Google Account security settings for third-party connections.
